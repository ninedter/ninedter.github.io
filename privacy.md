<h1>Privacy &amp; analytics</h1>
<p>This blog uses optional Google Analytics 4 to understand readership: article views, visits, traffic sources, approximate location, device categories, and reading interactions such as scrolling and outbound links.</p>
<p>Google Analytics loads only after you select “Allow analytics.” Selecting “No thanks” keeps it off. Your choice is saved in your browser. You can change it here at any time; declining stops future collection and removes this site's Analytics cookies. It does not delete data already sent.</p>
<p><button type="button" data-analytics-settings>Change analytics preference</button></p>
<p>When enabled, Google receives usage information and sets Analytics cookies to distinguish visits. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in this site's tag. This blog does not send names, email addresses, or a user ID to Analytics. Page URLs sent by the tag omit query strings and fragments; automatic site-search and form-interaction tracking are disabled.</p>
<p>Read <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">how Google uses information from sites that use its services</a> and <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google's privacy policy</a>.</p>
<p>The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information when serving the site, independently of your Analytics choice; see <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">GitHub's privacy statement</a>. Articles may also include credited images hosted by their original publishers.</p>
